# Motion sting

Expose `window.__hm = { duration, seek(t) }`.
2 to 3 motions max. Opacity and translateY. No bounce.
`npx html-marketing video path.html -o out.mp4 --duration 4 --fps 30`
App preview size: preset `ios-preview-69` (886x1920).
