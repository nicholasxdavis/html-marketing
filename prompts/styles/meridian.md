# STYLE: Meridian

Light warm studio. Real dark device. Quiet luxury.

## Use when

Consumer apps that want calm and clarity. Health, notes, reading, lifestyle.

## Tokens

```css
.field-sand
--field: #F3EFE6
--field-2: #E7E0D2
--type: #14110E
--type-muted: rgba(20,17,14,0.62)
--accent: #0B3D2E
--bezel: #111111
```

## Composition

Portrait: `layout-caption-top`, dark `.phone`, bottom crop.
Landscape: `layout-split`, dark `.phone` or `.browser`.

Headline is ink, not white.
Shadow stays soft: 0 40px 80px rgba(0,0,0,0.18).

## Forbidden

Neon, black fields, loud gradients, stickers, serif novelty fonts.
