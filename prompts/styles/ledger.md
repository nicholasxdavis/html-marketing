# STYLE: Ledger

Serious product. Left-aligned type. Almost no decoration. Ink field.

## Use when

Finance, security, infrastructure, B2B, anything that must feel expensive and calm.

## Tokens

```css
.field-ink
--field: #0B1220
--type: #FFFFFF
--accent: #6AA8FF
```

Accent is used once: a single match state or a 3px rule. Never as a fill.

## Composition

`layout-split` or `layout-caption-top`.
Headline can be 2 lines, still 3-7 words total.
No blobs, no illustrations, no testimonial cards.

## Forbidden

Green Atlas field, playful tilt, outline phones, script type.
